# DECISION_RECORD_TEMPLATE

Use this template when creating a new decision record under `/decisions/`.

- Decision ID: YYYY-NNN (e.g., 2026-001)
- Title:
- Date:
- Owner:
- Status: Draft / Under review / Approved / Rejected / Superseded

1) Why
- Clear statement of the decision and its purpose.

2) Time horizon
- Expected duration (1 year / 3 years / permanent)

3) Alignment
- How this supports the Company Constitution and mission.

4) Customer impact
- Which customer(s) and how they are affected.

5) Evidence & sources
- List data, research, and links.

6) Assumptions
- Explicit assumptions and validation plan.

7) Alternatives considered
- At least two alternatives and trade-offs.

8) Risks & mitigations
- Top risks and mitigation actions.

9) Metrics & success criteria
- Measurable outcomes and monitoring.

10) Reversibility & rollback plan

11) Conflicts of interest

12) Approval & ownership
- Approvers (names/roles) and implementation owner.

13) Communication plan

14) Documentation & links

Sign-off
- Approved by: (name, role) — date
