# The Founder Blueprint

A practical, single-source workspace for founders and early-stage teams to turn an idea into a repeatable, investable company. This repo collects the playbooks, research, templates, and artifacts you need to define the problem, design the product, test go-to-market, and run the business.

Status: Early-stage — editorial & research scaffolding in place. Current branch: Claude-Worlspace.

## What is this project?
The Founder Blueprint is a structured repository for building startups methodically — from problem discovery through product, growth, operations, and finance. It organizes knowledge, experiments, and decisions so founders can move faster and reduce wasted effort.

## Why does it exist?
Founders lose momentum and signal when information, experiments, and decisions are scattered across notes, drives, and chats. This repo exists to:
- Centralize core company artifacts and playbooks.
- Make research, product choices, and metrics explicit and repeatable.
- Reduce onboarding friction for collaborators and advisors.

## Who is it for?
- Solo founders and founding teams planning product-market fit and early growth.
- Early employees (PM, marketing, ops, data) who need context and templates.
- Advisors, investors, and incubators who want a clear view into decision-making and progress.

## How this repo is organized
Top-level folders (each contains templates, notes, and README placeholders):

```
01_Company/      Company overview, team, legal, mission & values
02_Problem/      Problem statements, customer interviews, JTBD, validation notes
03_Strategy/     Business model, positioning, competitive analysis
04_Brand/        Brand guidelines, messaging, logos & assets
05_Product/      Product specs, feature backlog, wireframes, PRD templates
06_Research/     Market research, surveys, competitive research
07_Data/         Metrics & KPIs, tracking plan, datasets, analytics notes
08_AI/           AI experiments, prompt library, integration notes
09_Growth/       Growth experiments, acquisition channels, campaign results
10_Operations/   SOPs, onboarding, support processes
11_Finance/      Financial model, budgets, forecasts, fundraising materials
12_Roadmap/      Roadmap overview, milestones, release plans
Resources/       Templates, links, and cross-team references
Ideas/           Idea backlog and early concept notes
```

## How it fits together
- Research in 06_Research and customer notes in 02_Problem feed strategy (03_Strategy) and product decisions (05_Product).
- Product work is planned in 12_Roadmap and executed through feature specs in 05_Product while measurement and data live in 07_Data.
- Growth experiments live in 09_Growth and are tied back to KPIs in 07_Data.
- Company-level artifacts (policy, legal, team) are in 01_Company; brand assets live in 04_Brand; finance sits in 11_Finance. AI experiments and prompt playbooks are collected in 08_AI for reuse across product and growth.

## How to use this repo (short)
- Start in the folder that matches your current priority (Problem → Product → Growth → Roadmap).
- Use the templates in Resources to standardize notes and PRDs.
- Keep experiments documented with hypothesis, metric, date, owner, and outcome.
- Store canonical datasets and metric definitions in 07_Data.

## Contributing
- Add files or fill templates where you have direct knowledge.
- Name documents clearly with date, owner, and status (draft / in-progress / validated).
- Link experiments and decisions to the related roadmap item or KPI.
- For substantial changes, open a PR against the Claude-Worlspace branch and request review.
