# DECISION_FRAMEWORK

Purpose
- Provide a mandatory checklist and process for all major decisions affecting strategy, governance, products, research methods, partnerships, or editorial policy.

Scope
- Applies to board-level, strategic, and operational decisions recorded in the repository. Minor editorial edits are exempt but must still follow review guidance.

Decision checklist (must be completed before approval)
1. Why: Clear statement of the decision and its purpose.
2. Time horizon: How long this decision is expected to last (1 year / 3 years / permanent).
3. Alignment: Does it support the Company Constitution and the mission to be the most trusted intelligence company focused on Africa? (Yes/No) — explain.
4. Customer impact: Which primary customer(s) does this affect and how?
5. Evidence: What data, research, or sources support this decision? Attach links.
6. Assumptions: List explicit assumptions and plan to validate them.
7. Alternatives: Outline at least two alternative options and trade-offs.
8. Risks: Identify top risks and mitigation plans.
9. Metrics & success criteria: Define measurable outcomes and monitoring plan.
10. Reversibility: Can this decision be reversed or rolled back? What is the rollback plan?
11. Conflicts of interest: Disclose any COIs and how they will be managed.
12. Approval & ownership: Who is the decision owner, approvers, and implementers?
13. Communication plan: Who needs to be informed and how?
14. Documentation: Where the decision will be recorded (link to repository file or issue).

Decision types and approval thresholds
- Routine editorial or factual corrections: 1 editor approval.
- Product or data changes with commercial impact: product lead + research lead approval.
- Strategic or governance changes (e.g., mission, constitution, legal structure): owner approval + one independent reviewer (board-equivalent).

Process
1. Draft a Decision Record (use the checklist above) as a Markdown file in `/decisions` or as an issue tagged `decision`.
2. Attach supporting evidence and references; link related issues or PRs.
3. Assign owners and approvers; set a target review deadline (default: 7 business days).
4. After approvals, merge the decision record into the `decisions/` directory and update the decision log (DECISION_LOG.md).
5. If rejected, record reasons and next steps in the issue.

Decision log
- Maintain DECISION_LOG.md that indexes decisions with date, title, owners, status, and link to full decision record.

Review cadence
- Quarterly governance review to audit recent decisions, outstanding validations, and unresolved assumptions.

Enforcement
- If contributors bypass the framework for major decisions, maintainers may revert changes and require re-submission under the framework.
