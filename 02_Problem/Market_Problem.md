# Market Problem

Overview

Early-stage founders and small founding teams struggle to find and validate the real problems worth solving. Information is scattered across notes, chats, and ad-hoc documents, causing duplicated effort, missed signals, and slow iteration.

Target customers

- Solo founders and 2-5 person founding teams
- Early PMs/Founders inside startups (pre-seed to seed)
- Accelerators, advisors, and incubators who help early-stage companies

Customer pain points

- Fragmented documentation: customer interviews, experiment notes, product decisions, and metrics live in different places.
- No repeatable templates: each founder recreates research templates and PRDs, slowing progress.
- Weak experiment discipline: hypotheses, metrics, and outcomes are not consistently recorded.
- Onboarding friction: new contributors lack context and spend time rediscovering decisions.

Current alternatives

- Google Drive / Notion folders (unstructured, private, hard to standardize)
- Generic startup books and courses (high-level, not tailored to day-to-day execution)
- Consultants / agencies (expensive and time-limited)

Hypotheses to validate

H1: Founders will adopt a lightweight, opinionated repo structure if it saves them >3 hours/week over their current setup.
H2: Standardized templates for interviews, PRDs, and experiments will increase experiment completion and result in faster validated learning.
H3: A single source of truth for metrics and experiment outcomes will reduce redundant work among early team members.

Key metrics

- Time saved per week (self-reported)
- Number of experiments documented and completed per month
- Time from idea to first repeatable acquisition channel
- Adoption rate of templates within a cohort

Next steps for validation

- Run user interviews with 10 founders to quantify time lost to documentation friction.
- Ship starter templates to 5 early teams and measure experiment throughput over 6 weeks.
- Collect qualitative feedback on onboarding new collaborators using the repo.
