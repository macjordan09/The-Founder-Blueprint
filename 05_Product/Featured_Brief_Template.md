# Featured Brief Template

Use this template for the weekly featured brief in the Africa Intelligence Brief.

- Title
- Version & date
- Author(s)
- TL;DR (1–2 sentences: key takeaway + recommended action)

What happened / Key facts (sourced)
- Bullet list of events or data points with inline links to sources.

Short methodology
- Data sources: list + links
- Date ranges
- Transformations / filters applied
- Limitations & biases

Analysis / Implications (clearly labeled opinion)
- Interpretation of the facts
- Decision checklist: what should an investor/partner/founder do next?

Sources
- Full citations with provenance notes and snapshots where applicable

Caveats & uncertainties
- Confidence level (High / Medium / Low) and why

Author & contributors
- Names, roles, and any conflicts of interest
