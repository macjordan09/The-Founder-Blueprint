# Flywheel

Core idea

The project’s flywheel converts editorial signals into audience growth, trust, paid products, and higher-quality signals — creating a virtuous loop that accelerates both content quality and monetization.

Flywheel stages

1. Curated editorial (free newsletter & featured briefs)
   - Produce high-quality, source-tagged content that solves decisions for target users.
   - Outcome: attracts subscribers from investors, founders, and diaspora networks.

2. Community & contributor network
   - Subscribers and diaspora contributors submit tips, data, and local context; community members amplify content.
   - Outcome: more high-quality leads and sources for future briefs.

3. Signal capture & data assets
   - Build a registry of sourced datasets, citations, and reproducible analyses from briefs.
   - Outcome: reusable data assets that power premium reports and platform features.

4. Paid products (reports, advisory, membership, data)
   - Convert high-engagement users to paid offerings using targeted outreach and gated content.
   - Outcome: revenue funds deeper research and product development.

5. Product & data improvements
   - Use revenue to expand researcher capacity, invest in data pipelines, and build automation/AI tooling.
   - Outcome: faster, richer briefs and more compelling data products.

6. Signal feedback & distribution
   - Enhanced products and data drive better briefs which attract more subscribers and paying customers.
   - Outcome: accelerator effect — more reach, more sources, more revenue.

Key levers to accelerate the flywheel

- Editorial quality: invest in fact-checking and featured briefs to maximize trust and shareability.
- Contributor incentives: simple pathways (honoraria, attribution, access) for diaspora and local experts to submit leads and corrections.
- Data reuse: ensure every brief produces artifacts (datasets, templates) that can be repackaged into premium offerings.
- Conversion mechanics: gating high-value content behind trials, briefings, or memberships with personalized outreach.
- Sponsorship fit: align sponsor categories to audience segments to keep relevance and CTR high.

Metrics to monitor the flywheel health

- Subscriber growth rate and referral rate
- Fraction of subscribers who become engaged (open + click) weekly
- Number of source contributions from the community per month
- Number of reusable artifacts created per issue (datasets, playbooks)
- Revenue conversion rates by cohort (newsletter → report → membership)
- CAC payback period and LTV growth over time

Risks to the flywheel

- Editorial dilution: monetization or rapid scaling could degrade quality; preserve standards and editorial control.
- Contributor fatigue: low incentives may cause drop-off; rotate contributors and provide clear credit and small honoraria.
- Data bottlenecks: manual data curation can limit scale — invest in tooling to automate ingestion and cleaning.

Execution notes

- Start small: run the flywheel manually for the first 3–6 months (editor curates, researcher vocalizes provenance, manual outreach converts pilots).
- Automate and productize: after consistent signals, invest in tooling (dashboards, API, AI-assisted curation) to bring scale.
- Keep feedback loops tight: after each issue, log sources, contributor notes, and any paid conversions in a simple tracker to learn what fuels growth and paid conversions.
