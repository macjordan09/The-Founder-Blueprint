# Values

1. Customer Obsession — We start with customer problems and validate before we build. Listening is our default action.

2. Evidence-Driven — Decisions are experiments first. We bias toward measurable learning over opinions.

3. Iteration > Perfection — Small bets and fast feedback outperform slow, perfect launches.

4. Clarity and Context — Well-documented choices and outcomes prevent repeated mistakes and accelerate teammates.

5. Ownership — We ship what we commit to, and we own the outcomes, good and bad.

6. Humility — We seek critique, share failures, and learn publicly so others benefit.
