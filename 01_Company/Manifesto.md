# Manifesto

We believe building a successful company starts with disciplined curiosity and ruthless focus. Great products arise when teams deeply understand the people they're serving and relentlessly iterate on validated learning.

We commit to:

- Prioritize understanding over assumptions. Every major decision should be grounded in evidence.
- Ship small, measure fast. Rapid experiments beat grand plans that never meet customers.
- Share context openly. Decisions, outcomes, and learnings belong to the team and future builders.
- Optimize for customers, not vanity metrics. Growth follows value.
- Be pragmatic about craftsmanship. Move quickly, but leave things cleaner than you found them.

This manifesto guides how we document, design, experiment, and operate as we turn ideas into products and companies.
