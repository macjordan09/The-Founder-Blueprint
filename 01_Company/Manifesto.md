# Manifesto

We are building a practical, evidence-first intelligence practice for African innovation, capital, policy, and enterprise. The Founder Blueprint exists to help decision-makers — founders, investors, corporate teams, and diaspora professionals — make better, faster, and more confident decisions about Africa.

Principles

- Trust over traction. Credibility is the most important asset: every major claim must be sourced, every dataset versioned, and every recommendation tied to evidence.

- Decision-first work. Every piece we publish must help someone make a better decision — whether that’s an investor evaluating a deal, a founder choosing a go-to-market channel, or a corporate team assessing a partnership.

- No hype, no gossip. We reject sensationalism and unverified rumors. We surface facts, label opinion clearly, and call out uncertainty.

- Source-first & reproducible. Prefer primary sources and reproducible methods. Save snapshots, record transformations, and publish a shortMethodology for featured briefs.

- Africa-led perspective. Center voices and expertise from African markets and the diaspora. Prioritize local context, credit contributors, and compensate expertise fairly when possible.

- Open, pragmatic, and iterative. We publish early, learn quickly, and iterate with real users. Openness means clear provenance and versioning; pragmatism means shipping minimal, useful artifacts that people can apply.

- Build the database from stories. Every brief is not just content — it’s an opportunity to improve our canonical database (companies, founders, investors, rounds, policies, deals). Content and data must reinforce each other.

- Editorial independence. Keep sponsorships, commercial pilots, and advisory work transparent and separated from editorial judgment.

- Human + machine collaboration. Use automation and AI to scale repetitive work, but keep humans in the loop for judgment, verification, and decisions that matter.

What we commit to do

- Publish a weekly Africa Intelligence Brief that is source-tagged, decision-focused, and reproducible.
- Produce featured briefs with clear methodology and DB actions that feed the Africa Database.
- Run disciplined user research with investors, founders, and diaspora professionals to validate needs and measure impact.
- Maintain an auditable chain of provenance for key facts and datasets, and correct errors transparently when they occur.

Call to action
